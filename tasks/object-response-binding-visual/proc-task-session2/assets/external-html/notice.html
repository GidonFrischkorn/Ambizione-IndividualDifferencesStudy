Online Experiment Notes

Before doing the experiment

  • If you need glasses or similar to perform the task, please put them on now.
  • Please turn off any music or videos now.
  • Please mute or turn off your phone and put it out of your reach.
  • Please make sure you won't be distracted during the experiment (e.g., your pets or your flatmates).

During the experiment

There are finger checks at the beginning of each task to check the finger positions on the keyboard, during practice to make sure that the instructions were read, and during the reaction time task in which a specific accuracy must be reached (i.e., 75%). Additionally, please do not leave this tab (your browser window) during the experiment and do not reload this tab! If you do switch tabs/windows during the experiment including the breaks (to check your mail or visit another website, for example), or you do not pass the checks, the experiment will end automatically and you will not receive credit for your participation. Repeated participation or resumption of the experiment is impossible.

After finishing the experiment

After finishing the experiment, you will automatically be redirected to the Prolific website. Refreshing or closing the browser window prematurely will make the allocation of your participation credit impossible.

I agree that I will not receive credit if I fail to follow the rules above.
